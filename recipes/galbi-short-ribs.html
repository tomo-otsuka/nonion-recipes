Allium-Free Galbi (Korean-Style Short Ribs)

A deeply savory, sweet, and tender adaptation of classic Korean short ribs, using daikon radish and Hondashi to achieve the traditional complex flavor without a hint of onion or garlic.

Yield 4 to 6 servings
Prep Time 30 mins
Marinate 2 hrs
💡
Flavor Swap: The Marinade Base

Traditional galbi relies heavily on pureed onion and garlic. To replicate this savory depth, we use a combination of daikon radish for its fresh, pungent bite, and Hondashi (dashi powder) to deliver the robust umami backbone.

Ingredients

The Meat

  • 3 pounds short ribs, cut in ½-inch slices across the bones (flanken-style)

The Marinade

  • ⅓ cup soy sauce
  • ⅓ cup brown sugar
  • ⅓ cup rice wine
  • 1 tablespoon sesame oil
  • 2 teaspoons black pepper
  • ¼ teaspoon cayenne or gochujang
  • ½ cup daikon radish, peeled and chopped
  • 1 tsp Hondashi (dashi powder)
  • 1 small Asian pear, peeled, cored and quartered (or use an ordinary pear or tart apple)
  • 1 1-inch chunk of ginger, peeled
  • 2 teaspoons sesame seeds
  • ¼ cup water

For Serving

  • Lettuce leaves
  • Sliced red or green hot pepper (optional)
  • Steamed rice (optional)

Instructions

Part 1: Prep & Marinate

  1. Rinse the ribs: Rinse short ribs in cold water, pat dry and place in a wide shallow bowl. In another bowl, mix together soy sauce, brown sugar, rice wine, sesame oil, black pepper, and cayenne (or gochujang).
  2. Make the purée: Put the daikon radish, pear, and ginger in the work bowl of a food processor. Grind ingredients to a smooth purée, then add to the soy sauce mixture. Stir in the Hondashi and sesame seeds. Thin the mixture with ¼ cup water.
  3. Marinate the meat: Pour the marinade over the short ribs and mix well, ensuring all pieces are coated. Cover and refrigerate for at least 2 hours, or overnight for best results.

Part 2: Cook

  1. Prepare for grilling: Bring the meat to room temperature. Drain and discard the excess marinade.
  2. Grill or broil: Cook the short ribs on a hot grill or under the broiler for 2 to 3 minutes per side, until nicely browned but still juicy.
  3. Serve: Pile the grilled meat on a platter and serve immediately with lettuce leaves on the side. Accompany with sliced hot peppers and steamed rice, if desired.
